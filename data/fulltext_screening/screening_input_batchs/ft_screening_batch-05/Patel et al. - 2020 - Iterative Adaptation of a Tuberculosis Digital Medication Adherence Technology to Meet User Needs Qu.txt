Participant 
selection  
  
  

10.  
Sampling  

How were participants 
selected? e.g. purposive, 
convenience, consecutive, 
snowball  

All providers at the training were 
invited to participate (no sampling). A 
convenience sample of patients from 2 
nearby health centers were included 
with an effort to purposely sample a 
range based on age, gender, HIV status, 
and treatment stage.  

11.  
Method of approach  

How were participants 
approached? e.g. face-to-
face, telephone, mail, email  

Clinic staff, who have a personal 
relationship with patients, approached 
participants face-to-face at regular refill 
visits to ask if they would like to 
participate.  

12.  
Sample size  

How many participants 
were in the study?  

52 providers and 7 patients were 
interviewed.
